# **App Name**: The Kings Pork House

## Core Features:

- Product Catalog: Display available cuts of meat with descriptions and pricing.
- Special Orders: Allow customers to request special cuts or quantities of meat.
- Recipe Recommendations: Suggest recipes based on the selected cuts of meat. The app will use an LLM tool to suggest recipes depending on the type of meat in question.
- Cut of the Week: Highlight a specific cut of meat each week with a promotional price.
- Contact Form: Provide a contact form for customer inquiries and feedback.
- Butcher's Blog: An editorial blog with tips, preparation guides, and stories related to meat.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Brick red (#B34747), a saturated, earthy tone reflecting the butchery's raw and authentic essence.
- Background color: Off-white (#F2EAE6), providing a neutral and clean backdrop to emphasize the richness of the meats.
- Accent color: Mustard yellow (#D4A846) to create a point of contrast for calls to action and special highlights, evoking tradition and craft.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', serif. Body font: 'PT Sans', sans-serif. A modern and elegant pairing.
- Use clean and modern icons to represent different cuts of meat and other features, like beef, poultry, and pork.
- Employ a grid-based layout for clear and organized content presentation with neumorphism styling, using soft shadows and inset effects to create a tactile feel.
- Use subtle transitions and animations to enhance user engagement, such as fading effects on product images and smooth scrolling.
